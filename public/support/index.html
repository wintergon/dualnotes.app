Common questions

Where are my backups?

In the Files app, in iCloud Drive › Notemono › Backups. Notemono makes one every week while Back up every week is on in Settings › Backup Files, and keeps the last four. You can also make one at any time with Back Up Now….

How do I restore a backup?

In Settings › Backup Files, choose Restore from Backup… and pick the file. Notemono adds what isn’t already on your device. Nothing already there is changed or removed.

My documents aren’t showing on my other device.

Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive on, and that Notemono doesn’t say Sync is off. If it says iCloud is full, nothing more goes up until there’s room.

Does Notemono collect my data?

No. Everything stays on your device and in your own iCloud account. The Privacy Policy lists exactly where each thing is kept.
